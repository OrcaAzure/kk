# Product

The Teletext Typhoon Mesh Portal (codename PAGASA-100) resurrects 1980s/90s analog Teletext (Ceefax) as an ultra-low-bandwidth emergency weather and road-advisory portal for Baguio and the Cordillera Administrative Region.

## Why it exists
During typhoons, 3G/EDGE is often the only remaining data path. Full dashboards fail. A 40×24 character grid with a sub-10KB JSON page loads on throttled links and can be read on a phone, netbook, or shared kiosk.

## Users
- Residents checking typhoon, rainfall, and landslide risk
- Drivers needing Kennon / Marcos Highway / Halsema road status
- Barangay / CDRRMO volunteers relaying official PAGASA and DPWH text

## Non-goals (v1)
- High-resolution maps or satellite imagery
- User accounts
- Paywalled or proprietary weather APIs as a hard dependency (mock feeds are first-class)
